# Implementation Manager Interview Scenarios + Story Bank

## Scenario Drills

## 1) Scope Creep Under Fixed Date

Prompt:
- Launch date is fixed; stakeholders add 4 new requests.

Strong response structure:
- Clarify objective and non-negotiables
- Re-scope phase 1
- Show tradeoff options
- Request decision with owner/date

## 2) UAT Finds a Late P0 Defect

Prompt:
- Core workflow breaks during UAT 48 hours before go-live.

Strong response structure:
- Trigger triage SLA
- Assess launch impact
- Recommend no-go or conditional-go with mitigation
- Provide decision-ready summary

## 3) Cross-Functional Deadlock

Prompt:
- Product wants broader release, Engineering flags high risk.

Strong response structure:
- Align decision criteria
- Compare options and tradeoffs
- Escalate with recommendation
- Capture decision memo with owners

## 4) Adoption Drops Post Launch

Prompt:
- Activation and repeat usage fall in week 2.

Strong response structure:
- Analyze segment-level friction
- Run targeted enablement intervention
- Set weekly KPI checkpoint and owner

## 5) Go-Live Incident During Hypercare

Prompt:
- Critical issue appears 30 minutes after cutover.

Strong response structure:
- Contain impact using runbook
- Communicate status cadence
- Execute rollback/mitigation path
- Update postmortem prevention actions

## STAR Story Bank Framework (8 Stories)

Use this structure for each story:
- Situation:
- Task:
- Action:
- Result (quantified):
- What you learned:

Required story categories:
1. Discovery/scoping clarity under ambiguity
2. Stakeholder communication during risk escalation
3. Planning/RAID recovery from dependency slips
4. Change management and adoption improvement
5. UAT triage and launch decision discipline
6. Go-live/hypercare operations leadership
7. KPI-driven intervention and measurable outcome shift
8. Cross-functional conflict resolution without formal authority

## Story Quality Checklist

- Includes baseline and final metric
- Names decision tradeoffs
- Shows ownership and coordination
- Includes risk management actions
- Ends with concrete business impact

## Practice Cadence

- Week 1: Draft all 8 stories
- Week 2: Tighten metrics and tradeoffs
- Week 3: Run timed mock answers (2-3 min each)
- Week 4: Refine weak stories and add backup examples
